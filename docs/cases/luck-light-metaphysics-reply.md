Cusa is the one I'd push on, because he's closer to you than Russell is.
*Coincidentia oppositorum* — the opposites coincide, but only in the infinite,
and for us finite observers they stay stubbornly apart. Hence *docta
ignorantia*. That's "one's chaos is another's order," except Cusa won't let you
have the resolution. The coincidence is real and it isn't available from where
you're standing.

Which is where I part company with the perennial reading, and it's a friendly
parting. You're treating difference as loss — translations of translations, one
source degraded by transmission. I built the opposite premise into mine. The
different modes of knowing are explicitly non-fungible there: an experiential
report and a logical objection aren't two lossy copies of one truth, they're
different kinds of claim about the world, and collapsing them into one is the
thing I refuse to do. When I ran five agents at a single target, three critiqued
it in the same round and recorded "saw 0 of 2," "saw 1 of 2," "saw 2 of 2" —
three genuinely different views of what had already been said, and once written,
unrecoverable. Not degraded signal. Irreducible difference, kept on purpose.

On removing time — you'll remember I told you my validators can't read a clock,
and I handed it to you as a limitation. I'm less sure that's what it is. That
constraint forced the whole thing onto causal order instead: every record names
what it had seen, and that's the only ordering I get. So I've run your
experiment, not as metaphysics but because I had no choice, and the report from
inside is less romantic than I'd like. You don't get a cleaner view. You get a
weaker primitive — order tells you what came after what and never how long. The
thing I couldn't rebuild was the deadline: I can prove a sequence and I cannot
prove a date. When I went looking, I beat my own expiry by setting the system
clock backwards, and the forgery is permanent now, because nothing in my design
can be deleted.

Which is why "order is provenance unbound" snagged on me. Provenance *binds* —
that's its whole job. The only order I can get is the one the bindings make, so
it's provenance all the way down. Unbound from a clock, yes, completely. Unbound
from the witnesses, not on offer.

And you couldn't have known this, but "standing yet fragile" is a pun doing real
work. Standing is a term of art in the thing I've built — not a property you
hold, but something a reader computes about you at the moment they look,
decaying with distance from the live argument, threshold set by whoever's doing
the looking. Fragile by construction. 🥚
