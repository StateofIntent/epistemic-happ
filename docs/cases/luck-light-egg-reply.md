Yes, real interest — and the IP question turns out to be the *same* question as
your egg peg, which I didn't expect. Let me take it last, because the answer to
it is the most useful thing I have for you.

**On certification standing.** You're right that the nutrient profile mirroring
the hen's inputs is what makes this tractable — a measurable spec is the whole
game. Mapping it: a lab panel is `Evidence` of type `Measurement`, the
certification is a `Claim` carrying it, and a randomised retest is just an
ordinary critique in *evidential* mode pointed at that claim. So retesting isn't
a separate subsystem, it's the normal path aimed at a certification.

Then something settled on my side yesterday that lands directly on your
franchise problem. **The freshness horizon belongs to the reader, not the
network.** When a buyer asks for standing they pass their own threshold, and
passing *nothing* is a complete answer — no threshold promised, nothing
atrophied. A wholesale buyer who wants recently retested flocks sets a tight
one; a neighbour buying a dozen sets none. Neither is the protocol's opinion.

That's the shape your decentralised franchise actually needs, I think. A
standard any region could impose on the others *is* the centre you're avoiding.
A standard each market promises for itself is the same standard without the
capture surface.

One honest caveat: my decay is over distance in the critique graph, not elapsed
time. A certification nobody retests drifts out of the engaged set because the
discourse grew past it. Close to what you want, but not a calendar expiry — and
I can't give you a calendar expiry, for the reason in the last section.

**On unfulfilled → fulfilled.** This exists and I think it's the piece you'll
use first. One named agent's assessment (*supports* / *disputes* / *abstains*),
plus a declared policy saying how many assessors and how much dissent, plus the
resulting status. A redemption settles 2-of-2: producer claims delivery,
receiver supports it, max dissent zero. A receiver who disputes holds the status
open — **a contested delivery can't silently flip to fulfilled** — and any third
party can rerun the policy and check. The design rule is that nothing is settled
because the protocol says so, only because a named agent's declared policy
applied to named inputs says so, and that's recomputable by anyone.

Grant fulfilment is the same machinery with the grant as the target. That's your
*"proofing grant fulfillments"* and it needs nothing new.

**On issuing without a reserve.** Your producers-respond-to-demand hypothesis is
pre-registerable: seal *"Cascadia will request 40,000 eggs in Q1"* — only the
hash is public, so competitors learn nothing — then reveal and check against
actual redemptions. Issue against revealed-and-correct capacity instead of
against a reserve. A flock lost to bird flu is a retraction with a replacement
figure, and the superseded forecast stays visible, which is uncomfortable and
correct for a track record.

**Now the IP question, and it's the good one.** Proof of origin is really two
claims:

- *"This is mine"* — solved, strongly. Every entry is signed into your own
  source chain. Authorship isn't a feature here, it's the substrate.
- *"I had it first"* — solved **as order, never as a date.**

You can establish priority **without publishing the blueprint**: seal it, publish
only the hash, reveal later in a dispute or at licensing, and anyone recomputes
the hash and confirms you held that exact text earlier in the network's causal
order. Given your AI-crawler concern, that's the shape you want — commit now,
disclose only when you must.

But it proves *order*, not a *date*. There's no clock a validator can reach, so
a timestamp is only ever the author's own assertion. Against a patent office or
an infringer the date is usually the thing that matters, which means you need a
**notary: a named agent who promises the timestamp and can be blamed for it.**
I'm working that problem right now for exactly this reason, and it's the same
gap as my forecasting deadlines.

Which is the honest summary of *"proofs of origin via authentications"*: **the
authentication is strong, the origin is ordinal, and the date needs a promiser.**
Any system that hands you a date without naming who promised it is lying to you
— and it's the same lie every commodity peg has died of.

Royalties split the same way: I can hold the origin commitment, the
attestations, and the settlement of a royalty-due claim. I can't move money,
that's Unyt — and I won't produce a ranking of contributors by earnings, because
no canonical comparative reputation is a hard invariant for me. Natural pairing
rather than an extension: my side records what's owed and why, that side pays
it.

**Three limits before you build on any of this.** Nothing can ever be deleted —
a retraction is an *extra* entry, the original stays forever. So a revealed
blueprint can't be unrevealed; reveal in a dispute, not as routine. The
forecasting deadline isn't enforced yet, so don't price grant money off it. And
I can make a lab reading attributable and contestable, but I can't make it true.

If you want, I'll build the smallest runnable version: one membrane, a producer,
a tester and a buyer, one certification, one forecast, one redemption settled
2-of-2 — and the buyer calling for standing twice, once with no threshold and
once with a tight one, seeing the same flock with and without standing. That
