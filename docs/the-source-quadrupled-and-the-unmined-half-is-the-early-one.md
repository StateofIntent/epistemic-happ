## 2. What §1–§10 contains that has no row here

Five items, each with the source's claim, where it lands, and what it would
cost. **None of them is an epistemics remedy; all five are design constraints.**

### 2.1 Loosening without backfilling is weakening, not growth

**`[Q]`** The source's §8.1 gives mammalian tissue growth a third term that
plant growth does not need:

> `growth = φ(P − Y) + matched deposition of new matrix into the strained state`
>
> *"Loosening without backfilling does not produce growth; it produces
> weakening. Same pressure, less material to carry it."*

**`[Q]`** It is not a metaphor there: irreversible crosslink inhibition is used
**as a model of aortic dissection**, at 50–70% aneurysm prevalence, and direct
enzymatic collagen cleavage carries a **boxed warning for rupture**. In plants
the two halves are *"the same program in the same cell, co-regulated and
co-localised"* — the wall *"never thins; it lengthens at constant thickness."*

**`[A]`** The transfer is to every relaxation this protocol has shipped or
considered: a gate dropped, a bar lowered, a refusal softened. **Each is a
`φ(P − Y)` move, and the question the equation forces is what carries the load
the constraint was carrying, deposited in the same place at the same time.**
This repository has a live instance in row 6.28's shape — a refusal whose
consequence is supplied rather than reversed (row 8.15) is the backfilled
version of the same move, and the equation says why that template is the right
one rather than merely the polite one.

### 2.2 The inhibitor must out-range the activator, or everything saturates

**`[Q]`** §7.2's synthetic Turing circuit in HEK293 has one control worth more
than the result: the **activator-only** arm *"formed no pattern — the signal
spread until every cell was positive. This is the same failure as a systemic
drug, reproduced in a dish."* Replacing the inhibitor with a variant carrying
the *activator's* diffusion range **abolished patterning entirely**. Measured
ranges: activator λ ≈ 28.7 µm, inhibitor λ ≈ 99.8 µm — **a 3.5× range ratio.**

**`[A]`** Stated for a claims protocol: **a mechanism that amplifies needs a
mechanism that damps, and the damping must travel further than the
amplification.** The question this poses here is empirical and has not been
asked: *does a critique reach every reader a claim reaches?* If critiques are
discovered by a narrower path than claims — a different link type, a different
query, a UI surface a reader can skip — then the protocol is the
activator-only arm, and the prediction is saturation rather than structure.
**Not measured in this session; it is the cheapest of the five to measure.**

### 2.3 Below a critical domain size, the structure does not form at all

**`[Q]`** §7.1: human ES cells on circular micropatterns, same cells, same
signals, different diameter — ***"Only the 1,000 μm circles formed all three
germ layers. The 250 μm and 500 μm circles did not."*** The source's gloss:
*"the dimensions of the confined domain determine whether the pattern forms at
all."*

**`[A]`** This bears directly on the pilot, and it is uncomfortable rather than
useful. **It is a threshold claim, not a gradient claim** — a domain below the
threshold does not produce a weaker version of the structure, it produces none.
The honest statement of what transfers is narrow: *if* a protocol's structure
requires a minimum number of independently-acting readers, then below it the
observable is indistinguishable from the mechanism not working, and no amount
of refining the rules moves it. **What this does not license is a number.**
The 1,000 µm figure is a fact about germ-layer patterning and carries nothing
about participant counts; asserting otherwise would be the cross-system
transfer the source's own §12.1b catalogues four times.

### 2.4 Compensation is gated, so many small pushes beat one large one

**`[Q]`** §11.1a-iv: *"compensatory proliferation requires a minimum insult
threshold to be triggered"* — under **35%** of cells perturbed produced
**neither** the local nor the systemic compensatory response. And the design
principle the source draws from it, which it flags as *"the first in this
review that follows from the architecture instead of from a mechanism"*:

| Strategy | Expected outcome |
|---|---|
| One large push on one factor | **triggers compensation** |
| Several small sub-threshold pushes across factors | **may escape it entirely**, and still compound multiplicatively |

**`[Q]`** The companion result is the one that makes it operable: **the tissue
defends the per-unit value and does not defend the total.** *"Impose a new
dimension and it manufactures units until the local variable returns to
setpoint."* Distraction osteogenesis works *because* the homeostat works.

**`[A]`** Two transfers, and the second is the stronger. First: a protocol
change large enough to be noticed summons the behaviour that cancels it, and a
set of small coordinated changes may not. Second, and this is the design
pattern: **do not fight the quantity the system defends — find the local
variable it defends and move the global condition so that local variable is
off its setpoint.** `[A]` The candidate instance here is conductance, which
row 8.8 already measures as a timer and 8.31 as a brake: a brake bounds a rate
and names no total, which is the same shape as a defended per-unit value with
an undefended product.

### 2.5 The only self-limitation you can engineer is the one built into the agent

**`[Q]`** §2.2 enumerates three ways an intervention self-extinguishes:
geometric self-limitation (the output removes the stimulus), boundary
exhaustion (the imposed condition is consumed), and **cargo half-life** (the
agent degrades by construction). The first two *"self-limit because of **where
the intervention acts**; both are inherited from biology's own architecture and
neither is tunable. The third self-limits because of **what the agent is made
of**, which makes it the only engineerable route to the criterion in this
corpus — the duration is a design parameter rather than a property of the
target."*

**`[V]` AND THIS REPOSITORY HAS THE FIELD AND THE WRONG DEFAULT.** `Promise`
and `Constitution` in `dna/integrity/src/lib.rs` each carry
`expires_at: Option<u64>`. Validation enforces it **only when it is present** —
the integrity zome's two checks are *"expires_at must be > created_at."* and
*"expires_at must be > published_at."* — and every read path treats absence as
permanence: the coordinator's trace and constitution reads both use
`expires_at.map_or(true, |exp| exp > now)`, so **`None` is valid forever.**

**`[A]`** So the protocol's one engineerable self-limitation is **opt-in, and
defaults off.** Whether that is wrong is a decision and not a defect — a
promise that expires by default is a different protocol — but the source's
argument is that this is the *only* self-extinguishing mechanism a designer
controls, and here it is the one that has to be remembered. That is the shape
§12 names repeatedly: *"a remedy that depends on the analyst remembering to
apply it has the same reliability as the judgement it was written to replace."*

## 3. The one §12 item this file claimed was missing — and it ships

**This section's first draft asserted that the source's *rotating
justification* finding had no counterpart here**, on the strength of a phrase
search returning zero hits for that phrase across `docs/`. **It is wrong, and
the way it is wrong is the thing worth keeping.**

**`[V]`** The finding is **row 8.37**, which quotes the same passage — a
proposal *"retained across four sections while its rationale was replaced
three times… with the ranking inherited rather than re-argued"* — and its arm
is **built and shipping**: `scripts/check-rationale-drift.mjs`, whose header
states the discriminator that makes it non-vacuous. The obvious version fires
on every row, because this repository's house style is to **append** to a row;
so the script flags a row only when a revision **deleted** a run of its
justification while the row's status signature was byte-identical across the
same revision. *"An append adds text and removes none. A REPLACED rationale
removes the reason it replaced."*

> **`[A]` So this file committed, inside an hour, the error the source names
> as costing it a ranked item: an absence claim scoped to the search and
> stated over the repository.** §12's wording is *"an unscoped absence claim
> converts a limit of effort into a property of the literature"*, and the cost
> there was that *"the spurious gap was ranked as a falsifiable item, so the
> error did not merely mislead — it allocated effort."* Here it would have
> proposed building a gate that exists.
>
> **The remedy that would have caught it is the one §12 lists as mechanisable
> and this file did not run: an absence claim must carry the scope it was
> searched over.** The defensible version of the claim was always available
> and costs one clause — *"the phrase does not appear in `docs/`"* — and that
> sentence invites the next search, `ls scripts/`, which settles it.

**`[V]` One observation left open rather than claimed as a defect, and this
one was measured rather than asserted.** There are **seventeen**
`scripts/check-*.mjs`; **fourteen are named in `.github/workflows/`**, and the
three that are not are **`check-load-bearing.mjs`,
`check-rationale-drift.mjs` and `check-stale-blockers.mjs`** — which are
precisely the three §8-derived report scripts. **`[A]`** All three are
report-style by construction (`check-load-bearing` prints its false-positive
classes rather than filtering them; `check-rationale-drift` reports its
denominator), **so this is not a gate that should be running and is not** — it
is a cluster worth noticing: the three checks this repository built from this
source are the three that do not run on a push. Whether that is right is a
decision, not a defect.

## 5. What does not transfer, recorded so it is not re-imported

**`[A]`** The source's **§11.1c-iv** result — *the reference is material, not
informational; every filled reference is a binding property of a protein, and
a material property cannot be changed pharmacologically* — has no analogue
here, because a protocol has no material layer. Its consequence does transfer
and is already row 8.43's: **changing how much of something there is gets
compensated; changing what the thing is does not.** The biology under it does
not.

**`[A]`** The **cube-root scaling** of §11.1c-vi (*"a 25-fold change in modulus
buys under a 3-fold change in length"*) is a result about elastic composites.
The portable half is the habit, not the exponent: **ask what power a lever
enters at before investing in it.** No row; it is advice, and §12's own
finding is that advice does not fire.
