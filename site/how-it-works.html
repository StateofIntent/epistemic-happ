Structure and process

Nothing to move

The cost of producing a plausible claim collapsed. The cost of checking one did not. Every proposed fix tries to sort the flood faster. This protocol removes the thing the flood is pushing against — and then makes disagreement a stored, typed object, so that checking accumulates instead of evaporating.

The attack

Generate ten thousand plausible claims. Or one claim, timed and framed to serve an agenda.

Why ranking fails

A ranked feed converts volume into position. The cheap thing moves the expensive thing.

The move here

There is no canonical score to occupy. Ten thousand entries produce ten thousand entries.

The alphabet

Nine kinds of part, and the typed edges between them

Every shape below is a real entry or link type in the integrity zome — the layer the network itself enforces, not a client convention. A reader needs the alphabet before the sentence, so this is the legend for all three figures.

Agent A keypair with a membrane. Not an account — nothing to suspend, nothing to score.
Claim An assertion stored with its author, domain, evidence and confidence. Never summarised away.
Critique A disagreement as a first-class object, carrying one of 5 modes. Colour = mode.
Evidence Typed: study, case report, video, image, text, measurement. Attaches to a critique or a claim.
SettlementStatus Open, Contested or Settled — recomputed from inputs and a policy pinned by hash, never declared.
Membrane A sovereign domain. Signals stay inside it; there is no cross-domain aggregate.
PreRegistration A public hash of a sealed prediction. The content stays private until it is revealed.
AntibodyPattern An agent’s signed recognition of a bad-faith shape: spam flood, sybil cluster, coordinated manipulation.
unexamined Grey throughout: a claim nobody has checked. Not false — unchecked, and the two are kept apart.

Figure 1 · the ordered mechanism

What a claim passes through, in order

Six stages, and the order matters: each one can only do its job because the one above it already ran. The right-hand rail is the thing that does not exist, and it is the reason the whole structure holds.

01

The boundary accepts almost everything, and that is deliberate

Validation checks that the author matches the signer and the entry is well-formed. It does not judge whether a claim is true — no code anywhere does. What it enforces is a per-agent rate brake: 20 critiques an hour.

That bounds how fast one identity can act; it does not bound totals, and the way past it is twenty-one agents rather than twenty-one critiques. The brake buys time, not safety, and the project records it that way.

02

A claim is addressable, so it can be argued with

The unit is the sentence, not the article. A reader can point at one assertion inside a long piece and attach a disagreement to that, which is what makes checking accumulate rather than scatter into comment threads nobody can cite later.

03

Disagreement is stored, and it has a shape

A critique is an entry with an author, a target and one of 5 modes. You cannot register that something is wrong; you must register how — the method, the logic, the sourcing, your own experience, or how it felt from inside.

Against a generated flood this is the expensive step, and it stays expensive. The difference is that the expense is paid once and kept, attached to the claim, visible to the next reader. A fact-check published elsewhere evaporates; this one is a link in the graph.

04

Evidence hangs off the objection, not the headline

6 evidence types, declared rather than inferred. A reader can see that a dispute rests on a measurement and the original on a press release — a comparison the reader makes, not one the protocol makes for them.

05

A verdict that can be recomputed is a verdict you can refuse

SettlementStatus is not a declaration somebody writes. Validation recomputes it from the inputs and a policy pinned by hash, so nobody can quietly restate last month’s verdict under this month’s parameters. 3 settlement states, and Contested is a real resting place rather than a failure to converge.

06

The reader is the governor

Standing is computed when somebody asks, and stored nowhere. There is no number the network maintains about you. That is why the struck box in the figure is empty: the question where is the score kept has the answer nowhere, by construction — a predicted absence rather than a missing feature.

Honest limits

What this structure does not do

Everything above is about what the shape makes impossible. These are the things it leaves open, and the first is live and unsolved right now.

  1. Discovery is the open problem, and a pilot participant has raised it three times. With no ranking there is also no summary, so a reader cannot see which claims have been critiqued without opening each one. Every surface that fixes this reintroduces comparison between neighbouring claims, which is the thing the design refuses. That trade is recorded, argued, and still undecided.
  2. The rate brake bounds a rate, not a total. 20 critiques an hour per agent is a brake: a single compliant agent is unbounded given time, and twenty-one agents clear the cap outright. It buys time for people to notice. It does not stop anything.
  3. Checking stays expensive. Nothing here makes verification cheap. It makes verification durable — paid once, attached, and still there for the next reader. That is a different claim, and a smaller one.
  4. Nothing can be erased. The permanence that defeats the vanishing-prediction trick is the same permanence that makes a deletion request impossible to honour. It is a deliberate property, and a real cost.
  5. Two operators is not a network. All of this is built and runs against a real conductor. None of it has been tested at the scale where flooding actually happens.

Provenance. Every type name, mode, state and threshold on this page was read from the integrity zome on 2026-11-26: 19 entry types, 31 link types, 5 critique modes, 6 evidence types, 3 settlement states, a cap of 20 critiques per window per agent, and 30 days of tenure before an attestation grant. Those figures are re-measured against the source tree on every push by scripts/check-site-claims.mjs, which fails the build when a number here stops matching the code.
